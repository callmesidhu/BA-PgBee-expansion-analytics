# 🐝 PgBee Business Growth & Expansion Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

PgBee is a student hostel discovery and booking platform designed to connect students with verified hostel providers.

This project analyzes PgBee's beta operations, evaluates financial viability, develops business intelligence dashboards, forecasts future growth, and recommends expansion strategies across Kerala and South India.

The project combines:

- Business Analysis
- SQL Analytics
- R Analytics
- Financial Modeling
- Forecasting
- Power BI Dashboarding
- Expansion Strategy

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

1. Analyze PgBee's beta performance.
2. Measure business and financial KPIs.
3. Evaluate profitability and unit economics.
4. Forecast future booking demand.
5. Identify expansion opportunities.
6. Develop executive dashboards.
7. Recommend growth strategies.

---

# 🏢 Business Context

Students often struggle to:

- Find verified hostels.
- Compare accommodation options.
- Access reliable information.

Hostel owners struggle to:

- Reach students.
- Fill vacancies.
- Market their properties.

PgBee solves these challenges through a centralized digital platform.
